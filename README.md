# Galaxy Quest · GQT

Dean Parisot's **1999 Star-Trek-pastiche comedy** as the **twelfth film-world** of **UD0**.
Live: https://davidwise01.github.io/galaxy-quest/

The washed-up cast of a cancelled Star-Trek-like show is mistaken by literal-minded aliens — the **Thermians**, who took the episodes as "historical documents" and built a real, working **NSEA Protector** from them — for actual space heroes, and must become their characters to survive the warlord Sarris. **Themed to its medium:** retro-Trek sci-fi TV — LCARS chrome, starfields, the Protector on the viewscreen.

Standing template — **The Arc · THE DOCUMENTS · Real or Fluff · The Message**. The deep-dive maps the **Star Trek: TOS pastiche** (Taggart=Kirk/Shatner, Dr. Lazarus=Spock/Nimoy), the "historical documents" conceit, the Dr. Lazarus / "By Grabthar's Hammer" emotional core, the superfan-saves-the-ship love letter to fandom, and the legacy: it **won the 2000 Hugo** (over The Matrix, The Sixth Sense, Being John Malkovich, The Iron Giant) **and the Nebula** for Best Script, and is embraced by the actual Trek community as one of the best Trek films. Justin Long & Rainn Wilson film debuts.
